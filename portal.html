Overview

Make a 20–45 second vertical video about the product — your face plus a screen recording of it actually being used. Follow the hooks and structure in the brief. Views are verified per post; your balance grows per 1,000 views.

Campaign guidelines

  • Original content only — record it yourself, face cam plus screen recording.

  • Show a real workflow. No fake earnings claims or fabricated screenshots.
  • Fresh account? Post 1–2 casual videos first so it doesn't read as spam.
  • Repurposing your own video into variants is encouraged — Remixa does it for you.
